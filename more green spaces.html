THE SOLUTION
🌳

More Green Spaces

Parks, trees and gardens that clean the air, cool the streets and make cities healthier to live in.

Concrete and traffic push city temperatures up and air quality down. Bringing nature back into neighbourhoods — through parks, trees and gardens — cools streets, filters pollution, and gives people a place to breathe, rest and connect.

Cooler streets

are created by tree cover, which can noticeably lower nearby air temperatures.

Cleaner air

results as trees and plants filter dust and pollutants from the atmosphere.

Better wellbeing

comes from easy access to parks and greenery close to home.

Key strategies

🏞️

Urban parks

Public parks give every neighbourhood a shared, breathable green space.

🌿

Rooftop gardens

Unused rooftops become gardens that cool buildings and grow greenery upward.

🌳

Street trees

Tree-lined streets shade footpaths and soften the hard edges of the city.

🥕

Community gardens

Shared plots let residents grow food and build stronger neighbourhood ties.

🛤️

Green corridors

Connected green paths let people and wildlife move through the city naturally.

🧱

Vertical gardens

Living walls bring plant life to dense areas with little ground space to spare.

What it looks like in action

Cities with strong green coverage share a few common threads:

  • A park or green space within a short walk of most homes
  • Tree-lined streets that offer shade in the hottest months
  • Rooftops and walls doubling as growing space
  • Community-run gardens that residents help maintain

Small steps you can take today

  • Plant a tree or a few pots of greenery where you live
  • Volunteer at or start a community garden nearby
  • Support local proposals for new parks and street trees
  • Spend time in existing green spaces to keep them valued and used
