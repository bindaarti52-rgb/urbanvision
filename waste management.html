Waste Management

Growing cities produce large amounts of waste, creating challenges for collection, recycling and disposal.

10 Problems Cities Face

Major urban problems related to waste management.

1

Poor Waste Segregation

Mixing wet, dry and other types of waste makes recycling and treatment more difficult.

2

Irregular Waste Collection

Irregular collection can cause waste to accumulate in streets and residential areas.

3

Overflowing Bins

Small or overloaded bins can cause waste to spill into public areas.

4

Open Dumping

Uncontrolled dumping can pollute land and water and create unhealthy surroundings.

5

Plastic Waste

Plastic waste can remain in the environment for long periods and enter drains and waterways.

6

Low Recycling

Valuable recyclable materials can be lost when proper recycling systems are not available.

7

Food Waste

Large amounts of food and organic waste require proper composting and treatment.

8

Landfill Pressure

Increasing waste generation creates greater demand for landfill space.

9

E-Waste

Electronic waste needs separate collection and safe recycling or disposal.

10

Worker Safety

Waste workers can face risks from sharp, hazardous or contaminated materials.
