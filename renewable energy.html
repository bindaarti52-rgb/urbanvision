Renewable Energy

Using solar, wind and other clean sources to power cities without relying on fossil fuels.

Cities consume enormous amounts of energy — for homes, offices, transport and industry. Shifting that demand to renewable sources cuts air pollution, stabilises energy costs, and keeps power flowing without depleting the planet's resources.

Sunlight

falling on a city's rooftops every day is enough to power thousands of homes if captured.

No fuel cost

is needed once solar panels or wind turbines are installed — the "fuel" is free.

Cleaner air

results when clean power replaces coal and diesel generation in city grids.

Key strategies

🔆

Solar power

Rooftop and community solar panels turn sunlight into clean, local electricity.

🌬️

Wind energy

Wind turbines on city edges and offshore generate power with zero emissions.

💧

Hydropower

Rivers and water systems can generate steady, reliable renewable electricity.

🏢

Energy-efficient buildings

Better insulation and smart appliances cut how much energy is needed in the first place.

🔌

Smart grids

Digital grids balance supply and demand, reducing waste and blackouts.

🤝

Community energy programs

Shared solar and subsidy schemes make clean power accessible to more households.

What it looks like in action

Clean-energy cities share a few common threads:

  • Solar panels on schools, offices and homes
  • Wind farms feeding directly into the city grid
  • Buildings designed to use less power overall
  • Incentives that make switching to renewables affordable

Small steps you can take today

  • Switch off lights and appliances when they're not in use
  • Ask your building or society about rooftop solar options
  • Choose energy-efficient appliances when you next upgrade
  • Support local clean-energy policies and community programs
