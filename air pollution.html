Air Pollution

Air pollution is a major urban challenge caused by transport, industries, construction and other activities.

10 Problems Cities Face

Major urban problems related to air pollution.

1

Vehicle Emissions

Cars, buses and trucks release pollutants into the air, especially in busy areas.

2

Industrial Pollution

Industrial activities can release smoke and harmful pollutants into urban air.

3

Construction Dust

Construction and demolition activities can produce large amounts of dust.

4

Open Burning

Burning waste and other materials can release harmful smoke and pollutants.

5

Indoor Air Pollution

Poor ventilation and indoor pollutants can affect air quality inside buildings.

6

Traffic-Related Pollution

Heavy traffic can increase the concentration of pollutants near busy roads.

7

Lack of Green Spaces

Fewer trees and green areas can reduce opportunities to improve the urban environment.

8

Industrial Smoke

Smoke and emissions from industrial facilities can contribute to poor air quality.

9

Dust from Roads

Dust from roads and uncovered surfaces can become airborne, especially during dry weather.

10

Health Effects

Long-term exposure to polluted air can contribute to serious health problems.
