Affordable Housing

Rapid urban growth can increase housing costs and make safe and affordable housing difficult to access.

10 Problems Cities Face

Major urban problems related to affordable housing.

1

High Housing Costs

Rising rent and home prices can make housing difficult for many families to afford.

2

Informal Settlements

Rapid urban growth can result in settlements without proper infrastructure and services.

3

Overcrowding

Limited affordable housing can force many people to live together in small spaces.

4

Unsafe Buildings

Poor construction and lack of maintenance can create unsafe living conditions.

5

Limited Water and Sanitation

Some communities may not have reliable access to clean water and sanitation facilities.

6

Displacement

Redevelopment can displace residents when affordable replacement housing is unavailable.

7

High Land Prices

Expensive urban land can make construction of affordable homes more difficult.

8

Distance from Jobs

Affordable homes may be far from workplaces, schools and important services.

9

Climate and Disaster Risks

Some low-cost homes may be located in areas exposed to floods, heat or other hazards.

10

Limited Inclusive Planning

Housing planning may not fully consider the needs of all sections of the community.
