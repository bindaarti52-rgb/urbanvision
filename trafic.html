Traffic

Rapid growth of cities can create traffic congestion, transportation problems and road safety challenges.

10 Problems Cities Face

Major urban problems related to traffic and transportation.

1

Traffic Congestion

Too many vehicles on roads can cause traffic jams and increase travel time.

2

Inadequate Public Transport

Limited buses, trains and other public transport can make travelling difficult.

3

Parking Shortage

Lack of parking spaces can cause vehicles to occupy roads and create traffic problems.

4

Road Safety

Speeding, poor road design and unsafe crossings can increase road accident risks.

5

Poor Footpaths

Broken or narrow footpaths can make walking difficult and unsafe.

6

Limited Cycling Infrastructure

Lack of proper cycling lanes can discourage people from using bicycles.

7

Poor Traffic Signals

Poorly coordinated traffic signals can create unnecessary traffic queues.

8

Poor Road Maintenance

Potholes and damaged roads can reduce safety and slow down traffic.

9

Road Encroachment

Illegal parking, street activities and encroachment can reduce available road space.

10

Long Travel Time

Heavy traffic can increase travelling time, fuel consumption and pollution.
